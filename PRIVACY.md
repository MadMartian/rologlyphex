# Privacy Policy

**Project**: Rologlyphex
**Last updated**: June 7, 2026
**Governing law**: Province of Alberta, Canada

## What Rologlyphex is

Rologlyphex is an open-source desktop utility that displays a visual overlay and synthesizes keyboard input on Linux systems. It is distributed as source code only and runs entirely on your local machine.

## Data we collect

**None.**

Rologlyphex does not collect, transmit, store, or process any personal information. The software:

- makes no network connections of any kind
- has no analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting
- has no user accounts or registration
- does not read, record, or transmit the content of your keystrokes to any external party
- does not communicate with any remote server

All processing occurs locally on your machine. No data ever leaves your system.

## Third parties

There are no third-party services, SDKs, or data processors integrated into Rologlyphex.

## Children

Because no data is collected from anyone, no special provisions for children are required.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be committed to the repository with a revised "Last updated" date.

Questions or concerns: open an issue in the project repository.
